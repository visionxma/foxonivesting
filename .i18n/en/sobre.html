About this site

This is Fox On's official website. Here you will find information about the platform, guides, the blog and our support channels.

What we do

We gather and organize public information about Fox On: what the platform offers, how account access works, its regulatory status and what risks an investor takes on. The goal is for you to reach a decision with more information than the broker's own sales page provides.

What we do not do

  • We do not give investment advice. We are not advisors authorized by the CVM, and nothing here is a buy or sell recommendation;
  • We do not promise returns. Any site that promises guaranteed gains in financial markets is lying;
  • We do not publish a made-up score or ranking. Without documented testing of our own, we do not assign a rating;
  • We do not trade on your behalf and we never ask for access to your account — if anyone does this in our name, it is a scam.

Corrections

Broker information changes fast: fees, assets, time frames and even jurisdiction. If you find something outdated or incorrect on this page, write to contato@foxonivesting.com.br — we will fix it and record the date of the change.
