Security and technology used

When it comes to crypto assets, platform security is one of the most important pillars for a worry-free user experience.

Fox On uses a range of technologies to protect its investors' data and trading activity.

Data protection with advanced encryption

The broker uses end-to-end encryption protocols, making sure all information exchanged between users and the platform is protected against unauthorized access.

That includes personal data, documents submitted for verification and transaction history.

Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication is an essential feature to make sure only the account's real owner can access it.

By turning on 2FA, investors add an extra layer of protection, making it much harder for anyone to break into or misuse their profile.

Internal and external audits

Fox On carries out regular audits, both internal and external, to identify vulnerabilities and ensure compliance with good digital security practices.

This process reinforces the company's commitment to reliability and operational soundness.

Fraud-prevention systems

Another important point is the use of anti-fraud technology, which monitors access behavior and suspicious transactions.

The platform uses algorithms that analyze activity in real time, helping prevent scams and protecting investors in every trade.

Transparency and regulatory compliance

Beyond digital security, transparency and compliance with regulatory standards are essential to building a relationship of trust with users.

Fox On follows practices that ensure clarity and legality across all of its operations, including:

  • Know Your Customer (KYC) policy: mandatory document submission to verify each user's identity, preventing financial crime and promoting a secure environment for trading.
  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) policy: strict controls over transactions to prevent the platform from being used for illicit activity, in line with international standards.
  • Transparency on fees and terms: the broker clearly displays the amounts charged on transactions, with no hidden costs or sudden changes to the terms of use.

Fox On operates under registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG) and aligns its processes with recognized standards, showing a commitment to legality and ethics in the crypto asset market.
