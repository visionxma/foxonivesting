What is the Fox On App?

The Fox On App is the official app for the Fox On platform, built to deliver a complete investing experience straight from your phone.

Available for Android and iOS devices, the app was created to serve investors of every profile, combining convenience, performance and security in one place.

With it, you can access the same features as the web version, with the freedom to trade wherever and whenever you want.

The app lets you trade assets, follow the market in real time, monitor your portfolio, set up alerts and manage your account with ease — all through a light, modern, intuitive interface.

The app is ideal for anyone who wants the freedom to invest without giving up professional features and smooth navigation.

Whether you're a beginner just starting out in investing or an experienced trader, the Fox On App gives you full control over your financial journey.

How does the Fox On App work?

The Fox On App works as a complete extension of the broker's platform, offering every essential feature for trading financial assets in real time.

It was designed to ensure convenience, speed and security throughout every step of your trading.

Here's how it works, in practice:

  • Simplified access: after installing the app, just log in with your registered email and password. New users can also create an account directly through the app, quickly.
  • Real-time market tracking: the app shows interactive charts across different time frames, with technical indicators and analysis tools for strategic decision-making.
  • Fast order execution: buy and sell assets with just a few taps on the screen, taking advantage of opportunities quickly.
  • Portfolio management: view your available balance, open positions, trading history and returns, all organized in one place.
  • Easy deposits and withdrawals: transfer funds into your account or request withdrawals directly through the app, safely and quickly.
  • Personalized alerts: set up notifications to be alerted when an asset reaches a certain price, without needing to watch the chart all the time.
  • Top-tier security: the app features advanced encryption, two-factor authentication (2FA) and protection against unauthorized access.

With this setup, the Fox On App keeps investors connected to the market at all times, with full independence to make decisions quickly and confidently.
