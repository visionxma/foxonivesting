Security and technology used

Data protection with advanced encryption

All personal and transaction data is protected by encryption protocols.

That means even as data travels across the internet, it stays out of reach of anyone with bad intentions.

Two-factor authentication (2FA)

The platform offers two-factor authentication (2FA), a feature that adds an extra layer of security to account access.

With it enabled, even if someone finds out your password, they won't be able to access your account without the second verification step.

Internal and external audits

To ensure full compliance and process efficiency, Fox On runs regular internal audits and also relies on independent external audits, reinforcing its commitment to security, traceability and transparency in its operations.

Fraud-prevention systems

The platform uses smart mechanisms to detect and block suspicious activity.

That helps prevent scams, intrusion attempts and irregular activity that could put a user's assets at risk.

Transparency and regulatory compliance

Beyond its security protocols, Fox On operates with transparency in its communications, terms of use and privacy policies.

The company follows the standards that apply to the industry, including anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorism financing (CFT) rules, promoting an ethical environment for all users. Fox On operates under registration in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (SVG).

This commitment to compliance not only builds public trust, it also shows that Fox On is aligned with global best practices in digital governance.
